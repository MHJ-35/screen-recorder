Screen Recorder

Record your screen and microphone. Works locally, offline, and free.

Browser support

  • This tool works best in Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.
  • It has been tested and works fine in Chrome, Firefox, Opera, and Edge.
  • Firefox does not support the MP4 video format. It will always save as WebM.
  • WebM files are larger in size, so MP4 is recommended when your browser supports it.
